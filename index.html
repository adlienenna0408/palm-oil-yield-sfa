🌱 The Bioscience Problem

In Malaysian palm oil plantations, tracking the cumulative weight of Fresh Fruit Bunches (FFB) per hectare as a palm tree matures is vital for supply chain forecasting.

Growth follows a Sigmoidal (Logistic) curve. Early years show slow growth, followed by an exponential surge, flattening out as the tree reaches canopy maturity.

Data Reference Model: Based on historical yield-by-age profiles mapped by the Malaysian Palm Oil Board (MPOB) ("MPOB Oil Palm Yield Profile & Statistics Data").

🧮 Mathematical Modeling

Let \(x\) be the age of the tree in years, and \(f(x)\) be the cumulative yield (tonnes/hectare).

Yield Function \(f(x)\)
$$f(x) = \frac{25}{1 + e^{-0.6(x - 6)}}$$
Derivative \(f'(x)\) (Rate of Change)
$$f'(x) = \frac{15 \cdot e^{-0.6(x - 6)}}{(1 + e^{-0.6(x - 6)})^2}$$

Calculated using the Chain Rule and Quotient Rule. It represents the exact yield addition rate (tonnes/ha/year) at any specific moment.
